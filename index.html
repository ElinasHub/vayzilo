Privacy Notice

Vayzilo is in early development. When you join our founding-host community or traveler waitlist, we collect the information you provide (such as your name, email address, city and state, number of properties, and your answers to optional questions).

We use this information only to contact you about Vayzilo’s development and launch, and to understand what hosts and travelers want from the platform. Form submissions are delivered to us by email through a form-processing service.

We do not sell your personal information. You can ask us to remove your information at any time by replying to any email we send.

This notice will be updated as the platform develops.
